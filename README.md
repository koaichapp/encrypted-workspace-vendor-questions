# Encrypted-Workspace Vendor Questions

**20 questions every workspace, messaging, or storage vendor should be able to answer** — a free, forkable security questionnaire for evaluating any tool that will hold your data.

Use it in a vendor RFP, hand it to your compliance team, or just ask the questions before you trust a tool with sensitive work. The full questionnaire is in **[VENDOR-QUESTIONS.md](./VENDOR-QUESTIONS.md)**.

> The single question most of these reduce to: **who holds the encryption keys?** If the vendor holds them, the vendor can read your content — whenever they choose, are compelled to, or are compromised. Everything else is detail.

## How to use it
- **Fork it.** Adapt the questions to your context; drop them into your procurement process.
- **Send it.** Paste into a vendor security questionnaire or RFP.
- A printable / save-as-PDF version lives at **<https://koaich.com/vendor-questions>**.

## What it covers
Key custody & recovery · vendor-side data access · AI & training · legal & transparency · breach posture · verifiability (audits, reproducible builds) · sub-processors · what happens on acquisition.
